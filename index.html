Channel publishing application

CompanionKnowWhy Publisher

A local tool that prepares educational videos and short teasers for the CompanionKnowWhy channel, then publishes them to the owner’s authorized accounts.

Watch the channel on YouTubeContact the operator

How the publisher works

  1. Prepare a video

    The channel owner creates the video, reviews its content and settings, and prepares a short teaser that points viewers to the full story.

  2. Connect the channel accounts

    The owner authorizes YouTube, the Youshouldknow Facebook Page, and Instagram @companionknowwhy through their platforms. Account checks verify the intended destinations.

  3. Publish and record the result

    The publisher checks that the full YouTube video is public before releasing its teasers. Local records track completed uploads and help prevent duplicates.

For the channel owner

This is an owner-operated desktop application. This website provides information and policies; it does not offer account connections, uploads, or public registration.

The app uses YouTube API Services and Meta APIs. Platform authorization and upload eligibility are separate requirements; this page does not indicate platform approval.
