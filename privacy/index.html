Data and account access

Privacy policy

Last updated October 1, 2026

CompanionKnowWhy Publisher is operated by the CompanionKnowWhy channel owner. It runs on the owner’s computer to prepare and publish the owner’s videos. It is not a public account-management service. Questions and data requests can be sent to companionyoutube@gmail.com.

Information the application uses

The publisher uses locally supplied videos, images, captions, titles, descriptions, and publication settings. When the owner connects accounts, it uses authorization credentials and account identifiers to access the selected YouTube channel, Facebook Page, and Instagram professional account.

Through the APIs, it reads account identity, linked-account information, video processing and visibility status, and caption status. It keeps upload identifiers, completion receipts, media fingerprints, and resumable-upload state locally so it can resume work and avoid duplicate posts. The application does not ask for or store a Google or Meta account password.

Why the information is used

Account information is used to verify the intended publishing destinations. Authorization credentials are used to upload videos, apply supported metadata, thumbnails and captions, and verify publishing results. The owner chooses the videos, destinations, content settings, and whether the publishing worker runs.

The publisher does not sell API data, use it to target advertisements, or send it to model-training services. Account API data is not an input to its video-generation models.

Storage and retention

In this installation, credentials and publishing state are stored in a private directory on the owner’s computer, outside the source repository and cloud-synced video folder. Operating-system file permissions restrict access. Video sources and exports stay in locations chosen by the owner.

The current application keeps local authorization and publishing records until the owner removes them; it does not provide automatic retention cleanup. Removing a publishing ledger also removes its duplicate-prevention history, so the worker must be stopped before those records are removed.

Sharing and platform services

The application uses YouTube API Services. Selected media, metadata and account requests are sent to Google/YouTube and, when selected, Meta/Facebook/Instagram for publication. Public posts become accessible under the visibility selected for the relevant platform. Local publishing credentials are not uploaded to this information website.

Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service. Meta’s handling of platform data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Revoke access or remove local data

  1. Stop the local publishing worker so it cannot make further requests.
  2. Revoke Google access through Google account permissions. Remove the Meta app connection through the connected Facebook account’s business integration settings.
  3. Remove the publisher’s saved credentials and account-derived publishing records from its private local storage, including any owner-managed backups. Revoking platform access does not itself erase those local files.

The channel operator can help with deletion or access requests at companionyoutube@gmail.com. Deleting local records or revoking authorization does not delete videos already published to a platform; those must be managed separately in the owner’s YouTube or Meta account.

This website

This information site does not include an account login, upload form, advertising, or analytics scripts added by the channel operator. Its hosting provider may process technical request information to serve and protect the site. Following a link to YouTube, Google, Meta, or your email application opens a separate service with its own policies.

Changes and questions

This page will be updated if the application’s data practices change. Contact companionyoutube@gmail.com with questions or concerns about these practices.
